Rubish

Type a letter or digit to get the moves that make a solved Rubik's cube show it on the front face.

How to read the moves

Start with a solved cube, white on top and green facing you. Keep it in that orientation the whole time.

U, D, R, L, F, B turn the Up, Down, Right, Left, Front, Back face a quarter turn clockwise, as if you were looking straight at that face. A prime (') means counter-clockwise; a 2 means a half turn.

A 3x3 face is tiny, so some characters share a shape (2/Z, 5/S, 9/Q). The center sticker can't move, so the letter color depends on whether the center is part of the letter.
